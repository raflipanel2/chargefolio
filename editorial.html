Editorial standards

How we research and revise battery-care information.

Primary sources first

We use public manufacturer information and safety guidance from organizations such as Apple, the U.S. EPA, U.S. Fire Administration and FAA. We link sources directly in each article when they support specific safety, recycling, travel or battery-use claims. Regional rules can differ.

No invented lab tests

Examples in our calculators and articles are explicitly hypothetical. We do not claim to have physically tested products, measured a particular battery, audited a charger or conducted accredited performance tests. Values are estimates with clearly written assumptions.

Separating fact from advice

An official safety warning is presented as a warning. A charging habit or efficiency assumption is presented as a suggestion or model. We avoid claiming one exact charge percentage or one accessory is best for every battery chemistry and device.

Corrections and review

Materials were substantively reviewed on 11 November 2026. We update wording and sources when changes are verified. Time-sensitive travel policies, recall status and manufacturer specs must always be rechecked at the official source. Please report issues through our contact page.
