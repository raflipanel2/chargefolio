LAB 02 / CHARGE EQUIVALENTS

Power Bank Planner

Model approximate full-charge equivalents using pack stored energy and a separately chosen device energy requirement. It does not measure delivered output.

A device may consume energy while charging, and low-voltage cutoffs and temperature affect results. This is a theoretical planning comparison, not guaranteed charges.

LAB 03 / LOCAL RECORDS

Charge Habit Log

Record observations such as charger changes, normal runtime or heat concerns. Avoid adding passwords, serial numbers or private identifiers. Entries are kept only in browser storage on this device.

Saved notes (0)

Browser storage can be cleared by private browsing settings or device cleanup. Export important records. This log is not a safety assessment; for smoke, hissing, swelling or damage, stop use and seek appropriate help.

Understand the assumptions.

Wh = (mAh ÷ 1000) × nominal V. Runtime ≈ Wh × efficiency ÷ W. Outputs change as real loads and batteries behave differently from the model.
