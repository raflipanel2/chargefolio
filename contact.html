Contact the editorial desk

How to reach ChargeFolio for questions, corrections and site feedback.

Email

Write to raflipanel2@gmail.com. You can send factual corrections with the article URL, a description of the error and a link to the relevant primary source. Please do not email device account passwords, private serial numbers or other sensitive personal information.

What we can address

We can correct unclear formulas, broken links, inaccessible controls, typographical errors and outdated public references. We cannot inspect a damaged battery over email or certify a charger for your device.

Urgent safety situations

Do not wait for a website reply if a battery is smoking, hissing, extremely hot, swollen or damaged. Stop use, keep yourself safe and follow local emergency and manufacturer guidance. We do not operate an emergency response service.
