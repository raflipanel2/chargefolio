About ChargeFolio

Independent, practical education about batteries and charging habits.

Why ChargeFolio exists

Battery advice online often conflates mAh with Wh, charger watts with capacity, and inconvenient aging with genuine safety concerns. ChargeFolio is a small independent educational website organized around those decisions, with practical calculators that show their assumptions and links to original guidance.

Who makes the content

Articles are prepared under the name ChargeFolio Editorial Desk. We do not claim to be an accredited battery laboratory, the device manufacturer, a repair shop or a government authority. We focus on explaining concepts in plain language and identifying what readers should verify independently.

Where we draw the line

We do not provide repair procedures for lithium cells, advice for overriding a battery-management system, or approval for hazardous-goods transport. Manufacturer instructions and competent professionals are necessary where electrical safety, damage or critical equipment are concerned.
