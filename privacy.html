Privacy policy

Information on local tools, analytics and advertising on ChargeFolio.

What you provide

You can browse the articles and use our calculators without creating an account. The Charge Habit Log stores user-entered device nicknames and notes in your own browser using localStorage. We do not run a server endpoint to receive those records. You can delete entries in the tool and clear browser site storage at any time.

Site hosting and network requests

The site is deployed through a hosting provider that may process access logs, IP addresses and diagnostic information under its own policies. We use external font assets and links to authoritative third-party sources; opening these may result in requests to those providers.

Google AdSense

We load the Google AdSense publisher-verification script on editorial pages. Depending on account approval, user location and configuration, Google and its partners may use cookies, similar identifiers or personal data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. For details consult Google advertising and privacy information. The site operator must implement any legally required consent management before serving ads to visitors in relevant regions.

Your choices and contact

You may block or delete site storage, use browser privacy controls and contact raflipanel2@gmail.com with privacy inquiries. This policy describes intended static-site behavior; if hosting, analytics or consent tools change, the policy must be updated.
